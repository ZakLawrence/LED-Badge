    Tips
    • Several designs on the badge: open one, then click + next to others in Saved designs (max 8 messages, 8 KB). The badge cycles through the messages.
    • Click/drag to draw; starting on a lit pixel erases. Right-drag always erases.
    • [ / ] previous/next frame, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+S save.
    • Animation mode: every frame is one 44-px screen. Other modes join frames left-to-right.
    • USB uploads are instant but the badge only shows content after unplugging. Bluetooth needs a button press per upload and takes ~1 s per 66 bytes.
